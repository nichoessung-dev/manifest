Clicks are counted per email and link, not per person, and no opens are tracked. Human clicks leave out mail scanners and link checkers: bot user agents, prefetches, clicks within 5 seconds of sending, two clicks from one network within 2 seconds, and known scanner networks. Rates are for the emails sent in each period. Warning and stop lines: complaints 0.05% and 0.1% over 30 days (3 in a day also stops), hard bounces 1% and 2% per send, unsubscribes over 1% per send, delivered under 98% and 95%, machine clicks over 35% and 50%. Mailbox groups come from the address on delivery, bounce, complaint and unsubscribe reports (only the group is stored); clicks have no address, so they have no mailbox group.

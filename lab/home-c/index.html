Good to know

Short answers to what people ask first.

Is it free?

Yes. Looking around, the buyer photos and the buy links cost nothing. You only pay for what you order, through your agent.

Is it safe?

You pay a shopping agent, not the seller. The agent buys the item, photographs your exact piece in its warehouse and only ships it after you approve the photos.

What is an agent?

A shopping service in China, like MyCNBox or KakoBuy. It buys from Weidian, Taobao or 1688 for you, checks the item and ships it to your country. Our Buy buttons open the exact listing in your agent.

How long is shipping?

Usually 1 to 3 weeks to your door, depending on the shipping line you pick. Sellers take a few days to get the item to the warehouse first.

Here's how to order your first finds from China through MyCNBox, in six simple steps.

Step one. Create your free MyCNBox account. Sign up through the red button on Puro Classico, and unlock up to five hundred dollars in new-user coupons.

Step two. Find something you love on Puro Classico, open it, and tap Buy via MyCNBox. The exact listing opens inside MyCNBox, so there's nothing to copy, paste, or translate.

Step three. Pick your size and colour, add it to your cart, and check out. Right now you only pay for the item, plus delivery to the MyCNBox warehouse in China. International shipping comes later.

Step four. MyCNBox orders from the seller, who ships it to their warehouse. When it arrives, you'll find real QC photos under Warehouse. Check the logos, stitching, colour and size tag. If something looks off, you can usually ask for a return or exchange.

Step five. Once your items have arrived, select them and submit packing, so everything ships together in one parcel. Choose a carrier, apply your coupon, and pay the estimated shipping. If the final cost is lower, the difference goes back to your balance.

Step six. MyCNBox ships your parcel within twenty-four hours. Track it under Parcels. Most orders arrive in about one to three weeks.

A few quick tips. Start with a small first order. Combine items into one parcel. And check the size chart, because Asian sizing often runs small.

That's it! Claim your MyCNBox new-user coupons, and start shopping at puroclassico dot com.

FAQ

Do I pay shipping when I buy the item?

No. At checkout you only pay for the item and delivery to the MyCNBox warehouse in China. International shipping is paid later, when you submit your parcel.

How long does delivery take?

MyCNBox usually ships within 24 hours of payment, and most parcels arrive in about 1-3 weeks depending on the shipping line and your country.

What are QC photos?

Real photos of your exact item taken at the warehouse before it ships, so you can check logos, stitching, colour and the size tag. If something is wrong you can usually ask for a return or exchange.

Can I use another agent?

Yes. Every find on Puro Classico also has links for KakoBuy, Oopbuy, LoveGoBuy and Sugargoo; the steps are the same.

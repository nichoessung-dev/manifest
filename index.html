Tutorials

New to buying from China through an agent? Start here.

1. How ordering actually works

Find something here, hit the buy link, and it opens on your agent (MyCNBox). The agent buys it from the seller, receives it at their warehouse, takes QC photos for you, then ships it internationally once you approve.

2. What "QC" means

Quality Control photos are pictures of your exact item taken at the warehouse before it ships, so you can check quality and flaws and either approve, ask for more photos, or refund.

3. Picking a shipping line

Cheaper lines are slower and riskier; premium lines (DHL/EMS) are faster and more reliable. Heavier hauls get cheaper per-kg, so consolidating a few items in one shipment usually saves money.

4. Rep glossary

W2C = where to cop (the buy link). Batch = a specific factory version of an item. GP = group purchase. Haul = a batch of items shipped together. 1:1 = as close to retail as it gets.

5. Staying safe

Order plain, avoid obvious branding on the shipping label, and use trusted lines for your region. Keep hauls reasonable in value.

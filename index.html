Buying from China through an agent – the complete guide

How buying from China through an agent actually works – QC, shipping, agents and more.

1. How ordering actually works

Find something in the catalog, hit the buy link, and it opens on your agent (MyCNBox by default). The agent buys the item from the marketplace seller on your behalf, receives it at their warehouse, takes QC photos for you to check, then ships it internationally once you approve. You pay the agent – never the site – and the whole flow is: find → agent buys → warehouse QC → you approve → ship.

2. What "QC" means and how to read it

Quality Control photos are pictures of your exact item taken at the warehouse before it ships, so you can check stitching, prints, logos, hardware and flaws. From the QC you can approve, ask for more angles, or request a refund. On Puro Classico, the QC tab on each product pulls real buyer QC photos so you can see the batch quality before you commit.

3. Picking a shipping line

Cheaper lines are slower and carry more risk; premium lines (DHL, EMS) are faster and more reliable. Heavier shipments get cheaper per-kilo, so consolidating several items into one haul usually saves money. Use the freight calculator in the Tools menu to estimate cost before you order.

4. Rep glossary – the words you'll see

W2C = where to cop (the buy link). Batch = a specific factory version of an item. GP = group purchase. Haul = a batch of items shipped together. 1:1 = as close to retail as it gets. QC = quality-control photos. Agent = the middleman who buys, stores and ships for you.

5. Staying safe and smart

Order plain, avoid obvious branding on the shipping label, and use trusted lines for your region. Keep haul values reasonable to limit customs exposure. Read the QC carefully – it is your one chance to catch a bad batch before it ships halfway around the world.

6. FAQ

Do I pay Puro Classico? No – you pay your agent directly. We only index finds and link out. Are prices exact? They track the live marketplace listing and can change. Why do some items have no QC? QC photos only exist once someone has already bought and inspected that item, so newer finds may not have them yet.
